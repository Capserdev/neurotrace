Conditions Screened

This tool screens for four neurological movement disorders that share overlapping clinical features.

PD
Parkinson's Disease

The most common movement disorder, characterized by tremor, rigidity, and bradykinesia. Affects dopaminergic neurons in the substantia nigra.

HC
Healthy Control

No neurological movement disorder present. Serves as the baseline comparison group for all diagnostic models.

PSP
Progressive Supranuclear Palsy

An atypical Parkinsonian syndrome causing balance problems, eye movement abnormalities, and cognitive changes.

MSA
Multiple System Atrophy

A rare progressive neurodegenerative disorder affecting the autonomic nervous system, balance, and voluntary movement.

How It Works

A two-modality assessment combined with an ensemble AI system for comprehensive screening.

🎙

Voice Analysis

Upload three short voice recordings (sustained vowel, text reading, vowel sounds). The system extracts 36 acoustic biomarkers — jitter, shimmer, harmonicity, formants, and nonlinear dynamics — and runs them through SVM classifiers.

4 Voice Models
✏️

Drawing Analysis

Upload images of standard clinical drawing tasks: Archimedean spiral, meander pattern, and sinusoidal wave. Deep learning models (EfficientNet) analyze motor control signatures in the drawings.

4 Drawing Models
🧠

Ensemble Diagnosis

All 8 model outputs are aggregated using a weighted ensemble algorithm. Results are presented as probability distributions across the four conditions with confidence indicators.

Weighted Ensemble

About This Research

This tool was developed as part of a Science Fair research project investigating multi-modal AI approaches for early screening of Parkinson's Disease and related atypical Parkinsonian syndromes.

The voice models were trained on acoustic recordings from patients with confirmed diagnoses of PD, PSP, MSA, and healthy controls. The drawing models were trained on digitized clinical drawing assessments.

This system is intended solely for research demonstration and educational purposes. It does not constitute medical advice and should not be used for clinical decision-making.

⚕ Medical Disclaimer

This AI tool is a research prototype. Results are not validated for clinical use. Always consult a qualified neurologist for diagnosis and treatment of movement disorders.
