Lifestyle Assessment

Clinically-validated questionnaire assessing motor symptoms, non-motor indicators, and risk factors.

Instructions Answer each question as accurately as possible based on your experience over the past 3 months. This questionnaire is a research screening tool only and does not constitute a medical diagnosis.
Motor Symptoms
Do you experience involuntary shaking or tremor in your hands at rest?
Has your handwriting become noticeably smaller or more cramped over time?
Have others told you your voice has become softer, hoarse, or monotone?
Do you experience difficulty with balance or a tendency to shuffle your feet?
Do you feel unusual stiffness in your limbs that does not go away with movement?
Non-Motor Symptoms
Have you noticed a reduced sense of smell or difficulty identifying common scents?
Do you act out your dreams while sleeping (kicking, punching, or yelling during sleep)?
Do you experience chronic constipation without a clear dietary cause?
Have you experienced persistent feelings of depression or anxiety that are new or worsening?
Do you feel dizzy or lightheaded when standing up quickly?
Risk Factors
Do you have a first-degree relative (parent, sibling) diagnosed with Parkinson's disease?
What is your age range?
